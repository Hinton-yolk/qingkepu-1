# 主持人角色 · 姿势提示词（试做 2 个）· 2026-11-10

用法：在 Gemini（选能生成图片的模型）里先上传你那张漫画头像，再粘贴下面的提示词。
- 两个姿势**放在同一个对话里**做：做完第一张，再发第二条，并加一句「和上一张是同一个人、同一画风」，一致性会更好。
- 脸、发型走样了就重新生成，不要将就；挑最像的一张。
- 生成后把原图（不要截图）发给我。我来抠图，缩成几毫米高，合成进测试镜头三的芯片和光纤画面，给你看搭不搭。

## 通用部分（每条都带上）
```
Use the attached portrait as the exact character reference: same face, same spiky black hair, same navy crew-neck sweater over a light-blue collared shirt. Keep exactly the same art style: hand-drawn ink outlines with watercolor washes and fine hatching. Draw the FULL BODY, from head to shoes, with dark grey chino trousers and simple dark shoes. Isolate the character on a plain, flat, pure white background: no paper texture, no brush strokes, no ground, no shadow, no text. Leave a small white margin around the whole figure. Portrait orientation, 2:3.
```

## 姿势 A：站着，指向一边（讲解时用）
```
Pose: standing, body turned three-quarters toward the viewer's right, his right arm extended forward and pointing at something off to the right at chest height. Friendly, slightly excited expression, mouth slightly open as if he is explaining something.
```

## 姿势 B：蹲下，看地上的小东西（尺度对比时用）
```
Pose: crouching low on one knee, leaning forward and looking down at something small on the ground just in front of him. One hand rests on his knee, the other hand reaches toward the ground. Curious, amazed expression with raised eyebrows. Same person and same art style as the previous image.
```

## 为什么这么写
- 纯白底：方便我抠图。纸纹和笔刷底色抠不干净，会在 3D 场景里留下白边。
- 全身、留白边：角色要站在电路板上，脚必须画全，否则没法做接触阴影，看起来像悬空。
- 不画地面、不画阴影：阴影我在合成时按场景的光来加，画死的阴影方向会和场景对不上。
