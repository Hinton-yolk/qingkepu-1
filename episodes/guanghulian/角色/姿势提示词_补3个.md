# 主持人角色 · 姿势提示词（补 3 个）· 2026-11-10

02 下集还要这 3 个姿势。用法同前：在**生成前两个姿势的同一个对话**里继续（或重新上传漫画头像和前两张图），每条前面带上「通用部分」。

## 通用部分
```
Same person, same outfit and exactly the same art style as the previous images (hand-drawn ink outlines with watercolor washes and fine hatching; navy crew-neck sweater over a light-blue collared shirt, dark grey chinos, dark shoes). FULL BODY from head to shoes, isolated on a plain, flat, pure white background: no paper texture, no brush strokes, no ground, no shadow, no text. Leave a small white margin around the figure. Portrait orientation, 2:3.
```

## 姿势 C：拍手（第 9 句「跟着一首歌拍手」）
```
Pose: standing, facing three-quarters toward the viewer's right, clapping his hands in front of his chest in rhythm, relaxed and smiling, head nodding slightly as if listening to music.
```

## 姿势 D：惊讶（第 6 句「四个，还是五个？」、第 12 句「拍子跑偏」）
```
Pose: standing, leaning back slightly in surprise, both hands raised near his shoulders with palms open, eyebrows raised and mouth open, looking toward the viewer's right.
```

## 姿势 E：站起来，正面（第 18 句「回到开头」，结尾）
```
Pose: standing upright and relaxed, facing the viewer, hands loosely at his sides, a calm confident smile, looking at the camera.
```
